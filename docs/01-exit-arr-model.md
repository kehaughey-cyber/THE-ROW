# Exit & ARR model

## Question
What must we reach, with how many members at what price?

## Findings
- [estimated] Required ARR by exit value (rows) and revenue multiple (columns), in $M, computed as exit value divided by multiple:

| Exit value | 5x | 6x | 7x | 8x |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $300M | 60.0 | 50.0 | 42.9 | 37.5 |
| $400M | 80.0 | 66.7 | 57.1 | 50.0 |
| $500M | 100.0 | 83.3 | 71.4 | 62.5 |

- [estimated] The founder's $300-500M exit target at 5-8x revenue implies roughly $37.5M to $100M of ARR; the table above gives each combination.
- [assumption] Members needed at three illustrative annual prices ($15k, $30k, $60k) for target ARR of $37.5M (low end), $50M (mid), and $100M (high end), rounded up:

| Target ARR ($M) | $15k | $30k | $60k |
|---|---|---|---|
| 37.5 | 2,500 | 1,250 | 625 |
| 50.0 | 3,334 | 1,667 | 834 |
| 100.0 | 6,667 | 3,334 | 1,667 |

- [assumption] ROW managers are free or near-free and carry zero ARR; a segment that does not pay contributes 0 even if it has a nonzero list price. Only paying members count toward the figures above.
- [assumption] The annual prices of $15k, $30k, and $60k are illustrative, not validated against willingness to pay.
- [estimated] Potential member counts from document 02 (market sizing), replacing the earlier illustrative counts:

| Side | Potential members | Pays? |
|---|---|---|
| Telecom operators (unique) | 2,000-3,000 | yes |
| Telecom contractors and engineering firms | 6,500-10,500 | yes |
| Telecom paying universe | 8,500-13,500 | yes |
| ROW managers (counties, municipalities, townships, DOTs, commissions) | ~38,840 total; 5,000-10,000 active | no (zero ARR) |
| Expansion paying universe (electric, gas, water operators and contractors) | 16,000-24,000 | yes |
| All-ROW paying universe | 24,500-37,500 | yes |

- [estimated] Share of the paying universe that must join to hit target ARR, computed as members needed (table above) divided by the low and high universe counts:

| Target ARR ($M) | Universe | $15k | $30k | $60k |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 37.5 | Telecom only | 19-29% | 9-15% | 5-7% |
| 50.0 | Telecom only | 25-39% | 12-20% | 6-10% |
| 100.0 | Telecom only | 49-78% | 25-39% | 12-20% |
| 37.5 | All ROW | 7-10% | 3-5% | 2-3% |
| 50.0 | All ROW | 9-14% | 4-7% | 2-3% |
| 100.0 | All ROW | 18-27% | 9-14% | 4-7% |

- [estimated] Reachability: the low end ($37.5M) is plausible but hard on telecom alone; it needs about 9-15% of all telecom operators and contractors paying $30k on average, and most of them are small firms (WISPs, rural telcos, local contractors) for whom $30k is unlikely, so it in practice needs a top-heavy tier where the roughly 20 national carriers and the largest contractors pay far above $60k. The high end ($100M) is not reachable at these counts on telecom alone (25-39% penetration at $30k is well above what a new association should plan for). With the electric, gas and water expansion, $100M needs about 9-14% of a 24,500-37,500 universe at $30k, which is ambitious but plausible over 5-10 years. Net: telecom is a credible beachhead for roughly $15-40M ARR; the exit range depends on the expansion and on data-subscription pricing to large firms.

## Assumptions
- Exit value range is $300M to $500M and the revenue multiple range is 5x to 8x, per the founder's target.
- ARR is all subscription revenue from paying members; ROW managers are excluded.
- Prices are illustrative and will be replaced once membership pricing research (document 07) is done.
- Member counts come from document 02 and rest on its assumptions (establishment-to-firm ratio, telecom share of NAICS 237130, overlap between associations).
- Penetration above roughly 15% of a paying universe is treated as unrealistic for a new association; this has not been benchmarked.
