# Membership & pricing tiers

## Question
What does each side get and pay?

## Findings

### How to read this document
- [assumption] Desk research dated 2026-11-04; no buyer has been asked what they would pay. Every price below is a proposal built from published list prices and from the value propositions in document 06, so each is labeled `[estimated]` or `[assumption]`. The only `[sourced]` bullets restate published comparables.

### Published comparables used
- [sourced] NTCA charges general broadband providers $592-$4,036 a year by employee band and ILECs up to $26,906, and its supplier and service-firm associate dues run $750 to $5,000 by employee band [S-026]
- [sourced] FBA network-operator dues are $1,200 to $6,000 a year by subscriber count [S-027]
- [sourced] FBA Premier (board-voting) membership is $9,500 a year [S-028]
- [sourced] FBA vendor membership is $6,000 a year [S-029]
- [sourced] NATOA charges agencies $925-$2,125 a year by population and for-profit associates $1,970 (2027 schedule) [S-030]
- [sourced] Shovels sells permit-data plans at $599 and $999 a month [S-032]
- [sourced] CGA dues run from $1,200 (Government/Municipality and Small Business) and $3,000 (Corporate) up to $100,000 (Platinum, 50 individuals) [S-057]
- [sourced] APPA publishes associate dues of $3,750 (corporate), $11,750 (elite corporate) and $1,075 (state or local government agency) [S-060]
- [estimated] Read together, these put association dues for paying firms at about $1,000-$10,000 a year, a self-serve data product at about $7,200-$12,000 a year, and the top of a multi-tier association (CGA's Platinum tier) at $100,000. The tiers below sit inside that spread; none of the comparables is a price for a predictive layer.

### Tiers
- [estimated] Proposed tiers. Prices are annual list prices; the mix column is the assumed number of members per 1,000 paying members, used in the blended price below. "Dashboard" means current and historical permit-time, fee and build data; "predictive" means modeled approval dates and build timing (document 06):

| Tier | Who | Newsletter | Meetings | Dashboard (current + historical) | Predictive layer | Annual price | Mix per 1,000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ROW Member | Counties, municipalities, townships, DOTs, commissions | yes | yes, with a seat in governance | yes, own jurisdiction plus peer benchmarks | no | free | not counted (zero ARR) |
| Operator Regional | Small operators (O3): WISPs, rural telcos | yes | yes | regional summary only | no | $2,500 | 250 |
| Operator Dashboard | Regional builders (O2) | yes | yes | full | no | $9,000 | 100 |
| Operator Enterprise | National carriers and MSOs (O1) | yes | yes, plus board block | full | included | $60,000 | 3 |
| Contractor Member | Local contractors (C2), small engineering firms | yes | yes | limited (build starts and permit times by region) | no | $3,500 | 450 |
| Contractor Dashboard | Regional contractors and engineering firms (C2, C3) | yes | yes | full | no | $9,000 | 190 |
| Contractor Enterprise | National primes (C1) and the largest engineering firms | yes | yes, plus board block | full | included | $40,000 | 7 |
| Predictive add-on | Any Dashboard-tier member | n/a | n/a | n/a | yes | $5,000 on top of the tier | 150 purchasers (about 52% of the 290 Dashboard-tier members) |

- [assumption] ROW managers are free and carry zero ARR. In the model they are a segment with pays set to false, so a nonzero list price would still contribute 0. If a nominal fee in the NATOA range ($925-$2,125, [S-030]) is ever proposed, it is excluded from ARR in every figure here: it would cover administration, not the revenue case. The proposal in this document is no fee.
- [assumption] ROW managers get the dashboard for their own jurisdiction and peer benchmarks in exchange for contributing permit records (document 06). The cost to the association is data collection and support, not revenue.
- [estimated] Operator Regional at $2,500: inside the $592-$4,036 band NTCA charges general broadband providers and the $1,200-$6,000 FBA band, and below the $7,200 a self-serve permit-data plan costs. Document 06 expects small operators to value meetings and a regional view, not an analytics seat, so a price near existing dues is the likeliest to be accepted.
- [estimated] Contractor Member at $3,500: between typical NTCA associate dues for mid-sized suppliers (up to $5,000) and below FBA's $6,000 vendor dues; contractors get a limited dashboard so the tier is a step above a pure newsletter-and-meetings dues payment.
- [estimated] Operator Dashboard and Contractor Dashboard at $9,000: set just under $10,000, the point where document 04 expects a purchase to be treated as a data/software buy with procurement review, and between the $7,200 Shovels entry plan and the $12,000 Pro plan. The same price on both sides keeps the offer simple.
- [assumption] Enterprise tiers at $60,000 (operators) and $40,000 (contractors) include the predictive layer and a board block. CGA's Platinum tier at $100,000 shows a top tier exists in a multi-stakeholder body [S-057], but nothing shows a telecom carrier would pay $60,000; the price is a placeholder to be tested.
- [assumption] The enterprise mix is 10 of every 1,000 members (1%). At 10,000 members that is about 100 enterprise accounts, which is a stretch against the roughly 20 national carriers counted in document 02 plus a short list of national primes and the largest regional builders and engineering firms.

### The predictive layer
- [assumption] Sell the predictive layer as an add-on at $5,000 on top of a Dashboard tier, and include it in the Enterprise tiers. Reasons: document 05 found that prediction is not unique (CostQuest already publishes a fiber-build model, and Shovels markets itself as a prediction engine), so the layer should not be the only reason to join; and it is unproven until an accuracy test exists (document 06), so an add-on lets a member decline it without leaving the association.
- [assumption] About 150 of the 290 Dashboard-tier members (about 52%) are assumed to buy the add-on, which is 15% of all 1,000 paying members. Only Dashboard-tier members can buy it, so the take rate among eligible members is far higher than 15%. Nothing supports that rate; it shows the add-on moves the blended price by $750.
- [estimated] A top-tier-only alternative (predictive only in Enterprise) would raise Enterprise appeal but leave the Dashboard tiers with no forward-looking reason to upgrade. The add-on is chosen for flexibility, not because it is shown to earn more.

### Value-adds beyond dues
- [assumption] Meetings sponsorship: operators, contractors and vendors may pay to sponsor meetings that ROW managers attend. NATOA's conference, webinars and newsletter show these products exist at associations [S-030], but no sponsorship price or sell-through was found, so none is counted in ARR.
- [assumption] Data licensing: selling aggregated, non-identifiable benchmarks to non-members (analysts, lenders, consultants). It depends on agencies agreeing that contributed data can be licensed, which has not been tested; none is counted in ARR.
- [assumption] Reports: an annual benchmark report sold to non-members. A comparable exists (CTIA sells an indices report for about $1,075 a year [S-052]), but no price is set here and none is counted in ARR.
- [estimated] Because these value-adds are unpriced and untested, the ARR figures come from dues and the predictive add-on only. Any value-add revenue would be upside.

### Blended price from the tiers
- [estimated] Computed with model/arr_model.py (arr, with ROW managers as a zero-ARR segment), 1,000 paying members at the mix above produce $6,020,000, a blended average of $6,020 per paying member. Without the predictive add-on it is $5,270. The blend sits in the $5,000-$10,000 band of document 01, near its lower end, because the mix is weighted toward small members.

### What must be validated
- [assumption] Willingness to pay at each tier, tested first with operators in two or three regions and a few contractors, before any price is published.
- [assumption] That ROW managers contribute data for free membership, with a signed data-contribution term from at least a few large jurisdictions.
- [assumption] That the shares in the mix (especially the enterprise tiers and the add-on take rate) can be reached; they drive the blended price more than any single list price.
- [assumption] That firms already paying NTCA, FBA or Shovels will pay a further $2,500-$9,000 rather than treat this as a substitute; document 05 names FBA and Shovels as the strongest threats.

## Assumptions
- All prices, tier contents and the tier mix are proposals, not validated willingness to pay.
- ROW managers join free and carry zero ARR; any nominal fee is excluded from ARR.
- Enterprise tiers ($60,000 operators, $40,000 contractors) can be sold to about 1% of paying members.
- About 150 of 290 Dashboard-tier members (about 52%; 15% of all paying members) buy the $5,000 predictive add-on.
- Sponsorship, data licensing and report revenue exist but are not counted in ARR.
